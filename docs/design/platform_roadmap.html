The gate before anyone else is let in

Opening Stoxsy to other people changes what it legally is. Pick the route first; everything else can be built and tested with made-up people meanwhile.

A. Registeras a SEBI investment adviser or research analyst. Full onboarding, suitability, agreement, records.
B. Partnerwith a registered entity that owns the client relationship; we receive only what we need.
C. Personal useonly you. No client onboarding is built.
