Stoxsy: Trader mode and Investor mode

Two different mindsets, so two different tools on the same honest foundation. Everything below is interactive. Numbers marked history are measured from your own loaded data (2016 to 2026); nothing here is a forecast or advice.

6. How a recommendation is produced in each mode

TRADER daily, after the close

1 Data checkfresh prices and F&O? else stop and say so
2 Regimecalm, neutral or risk-off: sets risk multiplier and exposure cap
3 Candidatesliquid names that pass the rules; risk screen from the defensive signal
4 Planentry, stop (in typical daily moves), target, time stop
5 Sizerisk budget / stop, capped by liquidity, correlation, open risk
6 Log and trackimmutable prediction, resolved by barrier rules; alerts on stop, target, time

INVESTOR at onboarding, then quarterly

1 Goals and profileamount, date, SIP, risk capacity and tolerance
2 Target mixprofile sets equity / gold / debt bands
3 Check holdingsdrift, concentration, overlap, data quality of each fund
4 Stress and rangeworst fall, goal probability range, sequence risk near the goal date
5 Actionsonly if a band is breached or the goal is off track: how much to add or trim
6 Review datenext check; alerts for drift and goal slippage
